## Which licence do you need?

You do **not** need a commercial licence to use ClipArchitect, including for
commercial purposes such as producing videos you sell or monetise. GPL-3.0
permits that. What GPL-3.0 requires is that if you **distribute** the software
or a work derived from it, you distribute it under GPL-3.0 as well, with source.

| What you want to do | GPL-3.0 | Commercial |
|---|---|---|
| Use the app to make videos, including for money | ✅ | ✅ |
| Study and modify the source for yourself | ✅ | ✅ |
| Distribute your modified version under GPL-3.0, with source | ✅ | ✅ |
| Embed ClipArchitect in a **closed-source** product | ❌ | ✅ |
| Redistribute it, or a derivative, **without** publishing source | ❌ | ✅ |
| Ship it under your own brand or as part of a paid product | ❌ | ✅ |
| Receive support with a response-time commitment | ❌ | optional |

The short version: **GPL-3.0 costs nothing and asks that you pass on the same
freedoms. A commercial licence is for those who cannot or do not want to.**

## Terms on offer

A commercial licence is negotiated per case. The terms it typically covers:

- **Commercial use and distribution by written agreement.** Uses that GPL-3.0
  does not permit — proprietary integration, closed-source redistribution,
  white-labelling — are granted only under a signed licence from the author.
- **No redistribution without a licence.** A commercial licensee may not
  sublicense, resell or redistribute ClipArchitect outside the scope agreed in
  writing.
- **Integration into proprietary products.** The licence waives the GPL-3.0
  copyleft obligations for the licensee, so ClipArchitect can be embedded in a
  closed-source application without that application inheriting the GPL.
- **Technical support and SLA, optional.** Priority support, response-time
  commitments and maintenance can be included; they are priced separately from
  the licence itself.

## What the licence does *not* cover

- **The videos you make with the app are yours.** Neither licence claims any
  right over the material ClipArchitect produces — output can be used
  commercially, published, sold or monetised freely under both options. Content
  you feed into the app (your footage, your scripts) likewise stays yours.
- **Third-party terms still apply to you.** ClipArchitect calls services you
  configure — LLM providers, Pexels, Pixabay, Azure, ElevenLabs — and bundles
  FFmpeg, fonts and music. Their licences and API terms bind you regardless of
  which ClipArchitect licence you hold. See
  [LICENSES-third-party.md](LICENSES-third-party.md).
- **Patent licensing for video codecs.** ClipArchitect encodes H.264 through the
  encoder built into Windows rather than distributing one. A licensee who
  replaces that with a bundled encoder takes on the AVC patent question
  themselves; the reasoning is documented in
  [LICENSES-third-party.md](LICENSES-third-party.md).

## Basis for dual licensing

ClipArchitect can be offered under two licences because the author holds or has
been granted the necessary rights to everything it ships:

- The desktop shell (`src-tauri/`) and the frontend (`src/`) are the author's
  own work.
- The vendored backend (`backend/app/`) comes from
  [MoneyPrinterTurbo](https://github.com/harry0703/MoneyPrinterTurbo) under the
  **MIT** licence, which permits relicensing and proprietary redistribution
  provided the copyright notice travels with it — a verbatim copy lives in
  [`LICENSE-MoneyPrinterTurbo`](LICENSE-MoneyPrinterTurbo).
- Tauri is MIT / Apache-2.0; the bundled FFmpeg build is **LGPL** and is invoked
  as a separate executable, so no copyleft obligation flows into the
  application; the bundled fonts are OFL-1.1, which explicitly permits bundling
  inside a commercial application.
- The bundled background music was generated by the author on a paid Suno
  subscription, under terms that assign the subscriber all of Suno's right,
  title and interest in the output. Note that Suno itself warrants no copyright
  in AI-generated audio, so these tracks are supplied as-is and without a
  warranty of ownership.

Every bundled asset's origin and licence is recorded in
[LICENSES-third-party.md](LICENSES-third-party.md). Anything whose
redistribution rights could not be established has been removed from the build
rather than shipped on an assumption.

Contributions from third parties, if any are accepted in future, would need a
contributor licence agreement before they could be included in a commercially
licensed build.
